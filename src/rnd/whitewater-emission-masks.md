## Goal

See what each emission driver of the Whitewater Source contributes on its own before mixing them.

## Setup

One FLIP splash, cached once. The Whitewater Source is cooked three times, each with only one driver on:

1. **Curvature:** crests and thin sheets.
2. **Acceleration:** impact areas.
3. **Vorticity:** churning water under the surface.

## Findings

Curvature alone gives the cleanest spray. Acceleration adds the punch at impact. Vorticity is the one to keep low, or the whole body of water starts to foam.
